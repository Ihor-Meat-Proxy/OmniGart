# Illustration asset licences

## OmniGart-authored files

All equipment and exercise illustrations under this tree are **original OmniGart work**, composed programmatically from reusable body poses and equipment primitives in `parts/` and emitted as SVG by `tools/`.

| Item | Licence |
|------|---------|
| `equipment/equip_*.svg`, `exercises/ex_*.svg` | Same as the OmniGart repository (AGPL-3.0) |
| `parts/**`, `tools/**`, `index.json`, generated previews / platform rasters | AGPL-3.0 |
| Contact sheets under `generated/previews/` | Derived from the OmniGart SVGs above |

No third-party illustration packs, stock art, or competitor assets were used.

## Rejected / not used

Evaluated and **not** vendored:

- Third-party exercise / gym icon packs (proprietary or non-AGPL-compatible)
- Photoreal stock photography
- Anatomical atlas tracings (body-map package already covers muscle highlighting via MIT MuscleMap paths; this package is a separate cartoon badge set)
