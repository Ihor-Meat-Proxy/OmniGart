# Glossary bibliography

Authorities cited in `glossary.csv` `source` cells. Prefer primary anatomical or federation wording over marketing copy.

## Anatomy

- **TA2 / FIPAT** — Federative International Programme for Anatomical Terminology. *Terminologia Anatomica*, 2nd edition. Used for scientific muscle names (`Musculus …` / local TA equivalents).
- National anatomical nomenclatures aligned to TA (Polish, German, Czech medical Latin/vernacular pairs) where those languages have established TA glosses.

## Strength & conditioning / exercise science

- **NSCA** — National Strength and Conditioning Association. *Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning* and related position stands (movement patterns, loading terms, safety: spotters, collars, Valsalva, progressive overload, RIR usage in resistance training).
- **ACSM** — American College of Sports Medicine. *Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription* and related statements (RPE, VO₂max context, mobility/balance, contraindications, warm-up/cool-down, endurance metrics).
- Core bracing literature commonly summarised in NSCA materials and clinical texts associated with lumbar-stability coaching (often cited as McGill bracing cues).

## Aquatics & athletics federations

- **World Aquatics** (formerly FINA) — stroke names and competition terminology (freestyle/front crawl, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly).
- **National swimming federations** — coaching vocabulary for drills and equipment (kickboard, pull buoy, paddles, fins).
- **World Athletics** — running drills, track/road contexts, easy/tempo run coaching language.

## Yoga

- Classical **Sanskrit** pose names as used in major teaching lineages (e.g. Iyengar / common modern postural yoga syllabi): Adho Mukha Svanasana, Balasana, Tadasana, Virabhadrasana I–II, Vrksasana, Trikonasana, Eka Pada Rajakapotasana, Setu Bandhasana, Urdhva Dhanurasana, Marjaryasana, Bitilasana, Anjaneyasana.
- Local-language glosses are teaching names, not alternative Sanskrit.

## Pilates

- **Pilates Method Alliance (PMA)** — established classical mat names: The Hundred, Roll-Up, Single-Leg Stretch, Double-Leg Stretch, Leg Circles, The Saw, Swan, Shoulder Bridge, Side-Kick Series.

## Language / orthography notes

- **Ukrainian** — sports-science and coaching Ukrainian; avoid Russian calques for muscle and lift names when a standard Ukrainian form exists (e.g. присід, жим лежачи, найширший м'яз спини).
- **Belarusian** — standard orthography (**Narkamaŭka**); classical Taraškievica forms are not preferred for this glossary.
- **Kazakh** — Cyrillic script only (қазақ әліпбиі); internationalisms kept in Cyrillic where that is normal gym usage.
- **Georgian, Polish, Spanish, French, German, Czech, Simplified Chinese** — prefer established coaching/medical terms; retain widely used Latin or English loanwords when they are the local gym standard (noted in `notes` when relevant).

## Heart-rate zones & watch workouts

- **Karvonen & Kentala (1957)** — heart-rate reserve method for target training heart rates.
- **ACSM** — *Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription* intensity categories (%HRR / %HRmax), warm-up/cool-down, aerobic training guidance.
- **Tanaka, Monahan & Seals (2001)** — age-predicted maximal heart rate (208 − 0.7×age) as an estimate alternative to 220 − age.
- **Seiler** — polarised endurance intensity distribution; Zone 2 as low-moderate aerobic work in coaching vernacular (descriptive model, not a medical protocol).
- **Lactate threshold / LTHR** — exercise-physiology coaching marker related to LT/MLSS testing; used here as a wellness training reference, not a clinical diagnosis.
- Platform workout scheduling concepts (warm-up / interval / cool-down / HR–pace–cadence alerts) mapped for localisation; keep wellness tone and short watch UI labels.
